Mission briefing · v0.9

Build a living Mars colony

Your mission is to establish a self-sustaining outpost, grow a population, and defend it against alien forces.

  • Build: choose a structure on the left, then click open ground. Press Escape to cancel.
  • Colony: habitats increase capacity. Colonists wander automatically. Farms and solar arrays produce resources each day.
  • Army: recruit infantry, then click an alien outpost to send your troops to attack it.
  • Survive: aliens patrol their distant outposts. Warning messages announce raids before they reach your colony. Defense towers automatically attack nearby enemies.
  • Camera: use WASD to move across the map, drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan.
  • Walls: select Wall Segment and press R to rotate it 90 degrees before placing.
  • Building info: hover over a structure for a short description.

This version runs locally in your browser. Three.js loads from a CDN, so an internet connection is required.
