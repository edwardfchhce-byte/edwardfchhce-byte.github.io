Development notes

From the devlog

What each project is aiming to become, and what is currently being prototyped.

Project 01 · Mars

A living colony

Citizens work automatically while the player decides where to build, what to research, and when to train and deploy a fighting force.

Project 02 · Divine

Choose a people, shape a war

Goblin numbers, human research, and elven individual strength create different strategic starts instead of one universally best race.

Project 02 · Dungeon

Loot fast. Extract alive.

The Ice King guards the deep route, while the sleeping Ice Dragon's awakening brings a spreading snow hazard that forces extraction.

Built around strategy, not spectacle.

Testing Out Games is a small prototype hub for systems-heavy games. The goal is to make choices matter: resources should have trade-offs, troop losses should mean something, and every expedition should create a story. Features are experimental and will change as the games are tested.
