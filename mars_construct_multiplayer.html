Mission briefing · v1.5

Build a self-sustaining Mars colony

You start with 1,800 credits and 220 materials. The guide establishes food, ore, processing, then coin production before asking you to fund an army. A nearby beginner outpost is intentionally weaker than the two advanced outposts.

  • Starter order: build a Hydro Farm, an Ore Mine, and a Processor. Click the Processor, then click the mine to connect them.
  • Earn credits: build a Trade Center and wait for it to pay at least 150 credits. Trade Centers earn credits every 20 seconds and may sell surplus food/materials.
  • Build an army: build a Barracks, click it to open training, and queue a Basic Rifleman (200 credits + 50 materials, 30 seconds). Use Deploy soldiers, then click open ground to move your soldiers.
  • First battle: attack the nearby Beginner Alien Outpost first. It has 90 HP and one weak 35-HP guard; advanced outposts have 180 HP and three stronger guards. Click an outpost to order soldiers to attack. Guards pursue nearby soldiers and deal damage; health bars show remaining HP.
  • Grow your colony: new colonists arrive automatically when you have spare housing, enough stored food, and nourishment of at least 70%. Keep food above three times your current population; arrivals are checked every third production day. A notification appears when someone joins.
  • Food and materials: colonists and soldiers consume food. Low nourishment reduces morale and can eventually cause colonists to leave. A Processor has no timer; it converts ore from its linked mine when that mine produces.
  • Research: after the first outpost, build a Research Lab to unlock technology upgrades.
  • Controls: select a structure on the left, then click open terrain. WASD moves the camera, drag orbits, scroll zooms, R rotates walls, and Escape selects. Hover over a building or build icon for its description.
  • Command Center: aliens do not attack the Command Center; they only fight soldiers. The Command Center has no production timer.

Three.js loads from a CDN, so an internet connection is required.
